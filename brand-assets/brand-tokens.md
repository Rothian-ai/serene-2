# Serene — Brand Tokens

Machine-readable brand system extracted from the Serene Brand Identity (Edition 01).
Use this as the source for brand RULES only — colours, type, logo, voice.
It is NOT a layout reference.

Brand name: **Serene** (never "Serene Bay"). Domain: serene.com.
Tagline: "Serenity, elevated." · Descriptor: "Real Estate & Curated Addresses."
Territory: Composed Luxury.

Notes:
- The mark is rendered in a **metallic silver finish** (Serene Silver), NOT flat gold.
- Wordmark is the word "Serene" set in Anek Latin Medium (500).
- Approved lockups: (1) mark stacked over the wordmark, (2) mark alone. Choose by background, never preference.
- Clear space: keep space equal to the width of one tower clear on all sides.
- Minimum size: 32px digital / 12mm print. Below that, use the wordmark alone (favicons, app icons).
- Don'ts: don't stretch, don't compress (keep tower ratio), don't rotate (skyline sits level), don't lower contrast (never gold-on-gold or ink-on-ink).
- If a crisp vector is needed at small sizes, re-trace THIS EXACT artwork to SVG — faithful, not a loose interpretation.

## Voice — every sentence must hold all three

1. **Composed** — never oversells; states what a place is, plainly. No exclamation marks, no "luxury lifestyle," no filler adjectives.
   - Write: "Floor-to-ceiling glass on three sides." Not: "Breathtaking views you have to see to believe!"
2. **Elevated** — confidence over volume; speaks upward, not loud.
   - Write: "Reserved for residents of Tower Two." Not: "EXCLUSIVE ACCESS — INVITE ONLY!!"
3. **Enduring** — built to be read in ten years; no slang, no trend words, no dates that expire.
   - Write: "A lobby that ages the way stone does." Not: "The most Instagrammable lobby in the city."
