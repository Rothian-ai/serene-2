## The Residence

Villas of five to seven bedrooms set directly on the crescent's sand, each with its own beach frontage and pool. Single-loaded plans keep every principal room on the water; the island's skyline sits across the bay, at a distance.

## Materials

Pale stone, timber shutters, deep verandahs: a coastal palette built for glare and salt, composed to age rather than to photograph.

## The District

The crescent is the Palm's outer arc, and its quietest. The beach is private, the water open, and the city precisely as far away as you choose to keep it.

## The Terms

An 80/20 plan with handover in Q1 2029. Villa beachfront rarely reaches the open market at launch. Amelia holds the release schedule and can flag comparable resales the moment you ask.
