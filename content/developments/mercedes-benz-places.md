## The Residence

Sixty-five storeys drawn from the marque's design language: a fluted silver form that catches Downtown's light differently by the hour. Residences of one to four bedrooms, with a crown of penthouses above the ninetieth metre.

## Materials

Brushed silver metalwork, smoked glass, saddle leather and dark oak: the marque's interior grammar, engineered rather than referenced.

## The District

Downtown remains the emirate's most liquid address: three minutes from the Mall, five from the Opera, and the fountain axis a short walk below. The tower stands on the district's quieter eastern edge.

## The Terms

A 70/30 plan with handover in Q2 2030, the longest runway in our register, and priced accordingly at launch. Amelia can model the construction-period appreciation case in a single question.
