## The Residence

Low-rise terraces in the cultural district of Saadiyat — eight minutes from the Louvre Abu Dhabi, four from the beach, all of it at the island's unhurried pace. One to three bedrooms arranged around shaded courtyards; the tallest building stops at nine floors because the site never needed more.

## Materials

Sand-toned precast, timber screens, deep overhangs. Architecture that answers the Gulf sun the way the region always has — with shade, mass, and patience.

## The District

Saadiyat is Abu Dhabi's long game: museums first, universities second, scarcity always. Supply on the island is deliberately finite, and the emirate has shown no appetite for changing that.

## The Terms

A 60/40 plan with handover in Q2 2027 — sixty across construction, forty on completion. Escrowed under ADRE regulation; Amelia can model the plan against current Saadiyat rental yields on request.
