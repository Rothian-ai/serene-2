## The Residence

Residences of one to four bedrooms in low-rise terraces around the marina — no corridor longer than a landing, no building taller than the masts. The quays below are lined the way Meraas lines them: a bakery, a bookshop, nothing that shouts.

## Materials

White render, timber pergolas, terracotta underfoot — the Mediterranean grammar applied with restraint, built for the light it sits in.

## The District

Port de La Mer closes the La Mer peninsula: beach on one side, marina on the other, the skyline across the water. It is the rare Dubai address where the car stays parked all weekend.

## The Terms

A 60/40 plan with handover in Q3 2028, at the most accessible entry in our register. Amelia can compare it against Bluewaters and City Walk yields the moment you ask.
