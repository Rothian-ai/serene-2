## The Residence

Residences of two to five bedrooms on the Palm's outer crescent, every principal room facing open water. The plans are Armani/Casa's own: long sightlines, low horizons, nothing that competes with the sea.

## Materials

Silk-rendered walls, pale stone, dark timber: the designer's palette applied without translation. The fittings are drawn from the Armani/Casa collection rather than specified to resemble it.

## The District

The outer crescent is the Palm at its quietest: beach on one side, the skyline held at a distance across the water. Six minutes to West Beach, ten to the Mall, and the city arrives only when called for.

## The Terms

A 60/40 plan weighted toward handover in Q4 2028, unusually buyer-favourable for beachfront. Amelia can model it against rental depth on the crescent in a single question.
