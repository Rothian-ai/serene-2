## The Residence

Garden terraces in Hartland II, built by the one developer in the emirate that employs its own trades. Two to four bedrooms over podium gardens; every residence with outdoor room enough to actually live in, not photograph.

## Materials

Sobha's backward integration shows where it counts: stone cut in-house, joinery from their own mill, metalwork finished by people on their payroll. Accountability, in material form.

## The District

Hartland II sits between the creek and Meydan: villas and low towers around a forested spine, ten minutes from Downtown without Downtown's density.

## The Terms

A 60/40 plan, handover Q3 2027, RERA escrow throughout. Ask Amelia for the finish schedule. With this developer, it is worth reading.
