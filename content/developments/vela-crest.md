## The Residence

Forty-two storeys of pale stone and low-iron glass above Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard. Residences of one to four bedrooms — each with floor-to-ceiling glass on at least two sides, and a plan that puts the morning sun where you have breakfast.

Ceiling heights hold at three metres throughout. Services run silent and unseen; the architecture does not perform, it endures.

## Materials

Travertine underfoot. Brushed brass at the hand. Oak where it matters. A palette chosen to age the way stone does — quietly.

## The District

Downtown remains the emirate's most liquid residential market: the district of the Opera, the Boulevard, and a rental depth that has survived every cycle since 2010. Vela Crest sits two streets from the fountain axis — close enough to walk, far enough to sleep.

## The Terms

An 80/20 plan through RERA-regulated escrow: eighty per cent across construction milestones, twenty on handover in Q4 2028. Amelia holds the full schedule, the service-charge projections, and the comparable resale record.
