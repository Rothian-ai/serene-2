## The Residence

The third tower at the harbour's quietest bend: thirty-one floors facing the creek, the sanctuary, and — on clear evenings — the Downtown skyline eight kilometres west. One and two bedrooms predominate; the corner three-bedrooms face the water on two sides.

## Materials

Low-iron glass, anodised aluminium, pale engineered stone. A lighter palette than Downtown — this is waterfront, and the architecture knows it.

## The District

Creek Harbour is the rare Dubai district still being priced. Infrastructure has landed ahead of population — the metro link, the bridges, the retail spine — which is precisely the window off-plan buyers look for.

## The Terms

A 90/10 plan with handover in Q1 2028. The heaviest construction-weighted schedule in our current registry — Amelia can set it against your liquidity preferences in a single question.
