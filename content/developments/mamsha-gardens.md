## The Residence

Maisonettes and simplex apartments directly on the Mamsha boardwalk — the beach on one side, the museums on the other. Ground-floor residences open to private gardens; upper floors trade garden for horizon.

## Materials

Honed limestone, bleached timber, bronze-anodised screens. The building is designed to be barefoot-first; everything at hand height is warm to the touch.

## The District

There is one beachfront row on Saadiyat and this is it. The island's masterplan caps the Mamsha strip permanently — scarcity here is not a projection, it is zoning.

## The Terms

A 50/50 plan with handover in Q4 2027 — the gentlest construction-phase load in the current registry. Amelia holds the beach-row resale history; it is short, and instructive.
