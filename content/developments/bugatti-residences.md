## The Residence

The tower's curves are drawn from the marque's coachwork: a sculpted-glass form that reads as movement held still. Two to four bedrooms, and a limited set of sky mansions; every residence is reached by a private garage lift that carries the car itself to your floor.

## Materials

Curved low-iron glass, brushed aluminium, leather and dark timber in the marque's own grammar. The interiors are engineered rather than decorated: every junction resolved the way a chassis is.

## The District

Business Bay has matured from back-office district to the canal-front extension of Downtown. The tower stands on the water side, four minutes from the canal run and six from the Boulevard.

## The Terms

A 70/30 plan with handover in Q2 2029. The branded premium is priced in from launch. Amelia can set it against comparable Downtown stock in a single question.
