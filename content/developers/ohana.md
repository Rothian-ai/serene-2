Ohana Development is an Abu Dhabi house working almost entirely in branded and customisable luxury: Ohana by the Sea, Ohana Hills, and beachfront residences carrying the Jacob & Co. and ELIE SAAB names. The group reports more than thirty-five years of activity across three continents and an asset value above eight billion dollars, with operations running from the UAE to Beirut.

The case is the branded-residence premium applied to villas rather than towers, where the customisation is real rather than a choice of two marble finishes. Branded stock in this market has held a documented resale margin over its unbranded neighbours. The trade is that the buyer pool for a customised beachfront villa is narrow, and a resale is an introduction rather than a listing.

Serene is registered with Ohana for its UAE beachfront portfolio. Handover histories and escrow filings for every Ohana development we present are available through Amelia, unedited.
