R.EVOLUTION is a European developer with roughly twenty-six years of work and twenty-three completed projects across Germany, Spain and Latvia. EYWA, on the Dubai Water Canal in Business Bay, is its first building in this market, and it is a single building rather than a pipeline: a heavily designed, biophilic scheme that treats the tower as one commission.

The case is a developer arriving with a finished European record instead of a launch narrative, concentrating it into one address on the canal. The counterweight is the same fact read the other way: one Dubai project, no local handover history, and a specification whose long-term running costs are not yet demonstrated by a completed year of service charges.

Serene is registered with R.Evolution for EYWA. Handover histories and escrow filings for the development are available through Amelia, unedited.
