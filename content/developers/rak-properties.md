RAK Properties was formed in 2005 under His Highness Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi with two billion dirhams of share capital, and its listing that year was oversubscribed many times over. It is a public joint stock company on the Abu Dhabi exchange, which makes it the rarest thing on this register: a developer whose accounts are filed quarterly and read by analysts who are not being sold anything.

Its register runs along one coastline. Mina Al Arab is a district of islands and protected mangrove, Hayat Island its most developed address, and Cape Hayat the current release. The case is a northern emirate priced below Dubai while its tourism infrastructure is still being built out, held by a developer that cannot quietly restate a delivery date without telling the market.
