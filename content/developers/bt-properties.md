BT Properties is the international arm of Bahria Town, Pakistan's largest private developer, and it opened its Dubai South head office in January 2025. The parent has spent three decades building master-planned cities at Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi, which is a different discipline from building towers: roads, parks, schools and utilities laid before the units are sold.

That is the case here, and the site suits it. Dubai South is a hundred and forty-five square kilometre plan around Al Maktoum International, the airport the emirate intends to move to, and WAADA is a mixed community of villas, townhouses and apartments inside it. The counterweight is plain: the UAE entity is new, registered with the Land Department as developer 2254 in February 2025, and has no Dubai handover record yet. The record belongs to the parent, in another market.

Serene is registered with BT Properties for its Dubai South releases. Handover histories and escrow filings for every BT development we present are available through Amelia, unedited.
