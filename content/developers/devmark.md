Devmark is on this register for a reason worth stating plainly: it is not a developer. Founded in 2018 and led by chief executive Sean McCauley, the group works as a development manager and sales consultancy, hired by developers to position a scheme, brand it, price it and take it to market.

That makes it a different kind of name to check. Where a developer's record is its handovers, Devmark's is the schemes it has taken to market for other people, and the escrow account behind any building it has marketed belongs to the developer, not to Devmark. If a Devmark name appears against a property you are considering, the question to ask is who is actually building it.

Ask Amelia for the developer of record behind any scheme marketed under this name, and for that developer's handover history and escrow filings, unedited.
