Meraas designs districts the way other developers design buildings. City Walk, Bluewaters and Port de La Mer are less projects than neighbourhoods, low-rise, walkable, curated down to the tenancy mix, and they have quietly become some of Dubai's most defended residential values.

The case for Meraas is texture. Its districts trade on how they feel to live in, not on tower-count, a rarity in this market, and the reason its resale premiums hold.
