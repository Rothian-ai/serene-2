Object 1 launched in the UAE in 2022 under founder Egor Maslennikov and has since put more than fifteen projects into the market, a rate of growth that reads as implausible until you look at where it built. Jumeirah Garden City and Jumeirah Village Circle are mid-market districts with real absorption, and EVERGR1N House, its biophilic flagship, sold out inside a month and ran to four phases.

The case is velocity in the part of the market that actually clears. This is not the branded-tower segment; it is well-specified stock at accessible entry prices, where rental demand is deepest and exit is a question of price rather than of finding the one buyer. The counterweight is a short record: a developer founded in 2022 has fewer completed handovers to examine than one founded in 1997, and that is the thing to check rather than assume.

Serene is registered with Object 1 across its Dubai residential portfolio. Handover histories and escrow filings for every Object 1 development we present are available through Amelia, unedited.
