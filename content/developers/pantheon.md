Pantheon Development, founded by Kalpesh Kinariwala, has built its register almost entirely inside one district. The Elysee towers and the Maison Elysee series are Jumeirah Village Circle addresses, and the concentration is the point: a developer that has delivered repeatedly in one community knows its absorption, its service charges and its rental floor better than a developer arriving with a first tower.

Maison Elysee 3, a 377-unit scheme, was launched with Savills Middle East, an unusual arrangement in which an international advisory attaches its name to the development rather than only to the sale. The case is competence in the mid-market at a price point where most supply is undifferentiated.

Serene is registered with Pantheon for its Jumeirah Village Circle releases. Handover histories and escrow filings for every Pantheon development we present are available through Amelia, unedited.
