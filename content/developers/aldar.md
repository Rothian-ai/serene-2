Aldar is Abu Dhabi's principal developer and, in effect, the custodian of its most deliberate masterplans: Saadiyat's cultural district, Yas Island's leisure economy, the Raha coastline. Where Dubai's market moves in waves, Aldar's moves by design: supply is released the way a museum hangs a collection, piece by piece.

That discipline is the investment case. Aldar districts are scarce by policy, not by accident, and the developer's balance sheet, among the strongest in the region, means construction finance never depends on presales momentum.

Serene Bay is registered with Aldar for its island residential portfolio. Handover histories and escrow filings for every Aldar development we present are available through Amelia, unedited.
