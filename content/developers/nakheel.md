Nakheel built the shoreline Dubai is recognised by. Palm Jumeirah remains the most literal expression of the emirate's ambition: land made where there was none, and held at a premium ever since. The developer's register runs from island villas to the waterfront districts still being released along the coast.

The investment case is geography. Nakheel controls coastline that cannot be repeated, and releases it slowly; a Nakheel address is, in effect, a fixed supply position in a market that keeps growing around it.
