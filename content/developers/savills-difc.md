Savills is not a developer either. It is an international real estate advisory founded in the United Kingdom in 1855 and listed on the London Stock Exchange, and Savills DIFC is its Dubai office. It advises, values, brokes and manages; it does not hold the escrow account or file the handover.

It appears here because the line between advising and developing has blurred in this market. Savills Middle East attached its name to Maison Elysee 3, a 377-unit Pantheon scheme in Jumeirah Village Circle, in a partnership where an international advisory lends its standards to a development rather than only to its sale. That is a useful signal about the building. It is not a substitute for the developer's own record.

Ask Amelia for the developer of record behind any scheme carrying this name, and for that developer's handover history and escrow filings, unedited.
